Privacy Policy

Learn how Alarm – Wake You Up handles your information, permissions, camera access, advertising, and privacy.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Welcome to Alarm – Wake You Up . We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may access or process, how that information is used, and your choices regarding your information.

01 · Information

Information We Collect

Alarm – Wake You Up is primarily designed to provide alarm, wake-up, and challenge functionality. Most alarm-related information is stored locally on your device.

⏰

Alarm & App Settings

Alarm times, schedules, repeat settings, sounds, challenges, and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

📷

Camera

Camera access may be used for camera-based wake-up challenges, such as eye-blink detection.

📱

Device Information

Third-party services may process technical information such as device and operating system information.

🔔

Notifications

Notification access may be required to alert you when your alarm is scheduled.

02 · Usage

How We Use Information

Information and permissions are used to provide the App's functionality, including:

  • Scheduling and triggering alarms.
  • Playing alarm sounds.
  • Providing vibration and notifications.
  • Displaying alarm screens.
  • Providing wake-up challenges.
  • Detecting completion of supported challenges.
  • Remembering your alarm and app settings.
  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Improving app stability and performance.
03 · Camera

Camera & Eye-Blink Challenges

If you choose to use a camera-based wake-up challenge, such as an eye-blink challenge, the App requires access to your device's camera.

Camera privacy:

Camera access is used for the selected challenge. The App does not intentionally record, store, or upload your camera video or photographs to our servers. Camera processing is intended to occur on your device.

You can manage or revoke camera permission through your Android device settings.

04 · Advertising

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

We do not control the data practices of third-party advertising providers.

05 · Third Parties

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide advertising, application functionality, analytics, crash reporting, or other technical services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • Other technical services used by the App
06 · Storage

Data Storage

Your alarm schedules, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on your device.

We do not intentionally maintain a server containing your personal alarm schedules or camera recordings.

Some third-party services, such as advertising or crash-reporting providers, may independently process information necessary to provide their services.

07 · Sharing

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may allow third-party service providers to process information when necessary to provide services used by the App, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or technical infrastructure.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.

08 · Security

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App and the services it uses.

However, no method of electronic storage, processing, or transmission is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09 · Children

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10 · Your Choices

Your Choices

You can control the permissions granted to the App through your Android device settings.

  • Disable permissions you don't want to use.
  • Manage notification permissions.
  • Manage camera permission.
  • Delete the App and its locally stored data.
  • Control certain advertising preferences through your device or Google settings.

Disabling certain permissions may prevent some App features from functioning correctly.

11 · Updates

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

12 · Contact

Contact Us
