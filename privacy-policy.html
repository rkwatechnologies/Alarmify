Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alarmify handles information, permissions, advertising, and camera-based wake-up features.

Last updated: September 3, 2026
Welcome to Alarmify – Smart Alarm Clock . We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how the application works. This Privacy Policy describes the information and permissions that may be involved when you use Alarmify.

1. Information We Collect

Alarmify is primarily designed to provide alarm, wake-up mission, volume control, protection, statistics, and related functionality.

Depending on the features you use, the application may access or process certain information and device permissions required for those features.

Alarm and App Settings

Alarm times, repeat schedules, selected alarm sounds, selected missions, theme preferences, and other application settings may be stored locally on your device to provide the application's functionality.

2. Device Permissions

Alarmify may request permissions that are necessary for specific features.

  • Notification permission for alarm notifications and related notifications.
  • Alarm and scheduling functionality for triggering alarms at the selected times.
  • Full-screen notification functionality where supported and required for the alarm experience.
  • Audio and vibration functionality for alarm alerts.
  • Camera permission when using the Eye Blink Challenge.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings.

3. Camera and Eye Blink Challenge

Alarmify includes an Eye Blink Challenge that can use the device camera to detect eye-blink activity.

Camera Privacy: Camera access is used for the Eye Blink Challenge when that feature is active. The feature is designed to detect the required eye-blink actions and does not intentionally require storing or uploading photographs or video recordings.

Camera access is not required for ordinary alarm functionality when the Eye Blink Challenge is not being used.

4. Advertising

Alarmify may display advertisements through third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect and process certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information related to advertising interactions, subject to their own policies and applicable laws.

Third-party advertising providers operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data practices.

5. Third-Party Services

Alarmify may use third-party services to provide advertising, application functionality, diagnostics, analytics, or other technical services.

Depending on the version of the application and the services enabled, these providers may process information according to their respective privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

6. How We Use Information

Information and permissions associated with Alarmify may be used to:

  • Schedule and trigger alarms.
  • Display alarm notifications.
  • Play alarm sounds and provide vibration.
  • Provide wake-up missions.
  • Operate the Eye Blink Challenge.
  • Provide volume and wake-up protection features.
  • Store application preferences and statistics.
  • Display advertisements where applicable.

7. Local Data Storage

Alarm settings, preferences, and certain statistics may be stored locally on your device.

We do not intentionally operate a server for storing your personal alarm schedules or private wake-up information.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services such as advertising or technical functionality.

Such third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application.

However, no electronic storage system or method of transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Alarmify is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

You can manage the permissions granted to Alarmify through Android settings.

You may also stop using the application or delete application data through your device settings, subject to normal Android system behavior.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, our practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Alarmify, you can contact us by email.
